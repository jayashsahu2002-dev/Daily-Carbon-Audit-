# Photo Checklist

Photos are the audit's main evidence, since we're not measuring anything. EAC 2018 clause 7.2(b) asks for an equipment inventory, and nameplates are what make those figures verifiable.

**How to upload:** on GitHub, open the `photos/` folder → **Add file → Upload files**. Name each file with the number below, e.g. `03a_AC_nameplate.jpg`, `06c_laptop_charger_label.jpg`.
You can also attach the photos directly in chat.
**Privacy:** crop or blur names, faces, room numbers and personal items. No people need to appear.

| # | What to photograph | Why it matters | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Room overview: 2–4 wide shots from the corners, including the window side | Shows the space and the fixtures | ★★★ |
| 02 | Window (glass, curtains or blinds, view outside) **+ a compass-app screenshot** taken facing out of the window | Shows orientation and solar gain | ★★★ |
| 03 | **AC nameplate or energy label** (model, cooling kW, rated input W, refrigerant type and charge kg) + front of the AC + display or remote showing the set temperature + the filter (open the front cover) | Needed for AC power, refrigerant (Scope 1) and maintenance findings | ★★★ |
| 04 | **AC card or meter display + top-up history screenshots** | Proves the HK$120/month baseline | ★★★ |
| 05 | Ceiling light fixture + tube/bulb label; desk lamp label | Lighting power | ★★★ |
| 06 | Label on **every plug-in device**: laptop charger, monitor, phone charger, mini-fridge, kettle, fan, hair dryer, router, etc. | Rated watts for the inventory | ★★★ |
| 07 | Power sockets or power strip with everything plugged in | Standby loads, and supports the "switched power strip" saving measure | ★★ |
| 08 | Shared washroom: **water heater nameplate**, showerhead, lights, exhaust fan | Hot-water energy is often the 2nd-largest load | ★★★ |
| 09 | Shared kitchen: overview, fridge energy labels, hob/microwave/kettle labels, any notices | Kitchen share of the inventory | ★★ |
| 10 | Laundry machines + labels or price list | Laundry energy | ★ |
| 11 | Hall exterior or name sign; floor plan or fire-escape plan on the door | Building characteristics and floor area | ★★ |
| 12 | Energy-saving notices, or wasteful conditions you spot (lights on in empty rooms, AC running with the window open, etc.) | Evidence for the walk-through findings | ★★ |

**Optional extras that improve marks:**
- A simple hand-drawn floor-plan sketch of the room with dimensions and the window position.
- A phone thermometer or hygrometer reading, or the AC display, at a typical time.
- The hall's published AC charging notice or rates page (a screenshot).
